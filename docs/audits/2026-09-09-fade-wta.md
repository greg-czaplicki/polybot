## Reading

- **The charter cohort is 5 rows total, ~4 per two weeks.** The would-have-bet
  cohort in WTA is tiny because the holder signal rarely clears all five gates
  in tennis (clean rate ≈ 5%, see `tennis-verdict.md`). At this rate n = 100
  is ~40 weeks away. Same "checkpoint unreachable" shape as the tennis verdict.
- **The earlier "−17% on 152 rows" figure was the raw probation-reason cut**,
  which includes rows failing other gates. The project's own rule
  (`docs/STRATEGY.md`, shadow-book vocabulary) says never read promotion stats
  from that cut. It is recorded here so the mistake is not repeated.
- **The naive fade sits at the charter's null.** Signal-side ROI is strongly
  negative, but the signal side in WTA is usually the underdog (mean price
  0.31). Fading it means buying favorites near 0.69, where a fade win pays
  ~0.45 and a fade loss costs 1. The asymmetry plus spread eats the inversion:
  −5.4% is exactly what the charter predicted for an uninformative signal.
- fade_pin_clv +0.62% on 46 rows is mildly positive but far from a
  criterion; pin coverage is 28%, below the 50% floor anyway.

## Decision

No fade policy is designed and nothing goes live. The instance stays open per
charter (one read at n = 100), but under the proposed CLV-primary verdict rule
with a DEAD outcome, the naive cohort at n = 166 would already close it.
Nothing about this is a statement about WTA tennis; it is a statement that
our holder metric has no usable information there in either direction.
